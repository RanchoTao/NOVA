# NOVA scientific contract v0.1

**A card is a falsifiable proposal, not a discovered result.**

## Required contract per card
1. Cite the *assumption being challenged* as a proposition to test, not necessarily community consensus.
2. Distinguish the intervention from the measured endpoint and identify the simplest alternative explanation.
3. State baseline(s), a decisive falsifier and at least two confounders.
4. Never infer novelty from a LLM confidence or lack of retrieved search results.
5. If a negative result occurs, preserve the exact idea/version and experiment evidence. A failed measurement is not a failed scientific hypothesis.

## Five-axis review by receiving project
- Science: does the hypothesized effect exist under an interpretable test?
- Measurement: is the experiment valid, powered and leakage-free?
- Novelty: is the exact claimed contribution beyond prior work?
- Execution: were genuine runs performed with verified resources and receipts?
- Paper: do the actual results justify a full paper?

## Explicit exclusions
No fabricated citations, invented results, self-certified Strong Accept scores, autopublish, automatic spending, claim resurrection or unauthorized modification of frozen experiment states. A historical-rediscovery benchmark is not valid without managing time-cutoff leakage from LLM pretraining.
